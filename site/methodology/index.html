Methodology and definitions

Reported stocks, derived ratios, source granularity and the limits on comparison.

Reported pending stock

A count at its stated date, not an annual flow. National pending adds only the three published court categories.

Concentration (derived)

Sum the three largest district/subordinate State/UT pending stocks, then divide by the sum for all loaded State/UT aggregates. Higher courts are excluded. This is not population-adjusted.

Over-five-year share (derived)

Reported cases pending over five years divided by reported pending. For combined figures, sum counts first; never average ratios. Zero denominators give unavailable.

Monthly clearance (derived)

Reported disposals divided by reported institutions. Zero institutions gives unavailable. Do not sum Total with Civil and Criminal components. Reported opening + institutions - disposals must reconcile where opening is supplied; no opening is inferred.

Unavailable measures

National monthly direction, national clearance and adjournment rates, and comprehensive individual district detail are unavailable in the national source. Missing counts are not zero.

Crime context is separate

Annual registered crimes are flows. Pending court cases are stocks. They are not equivalent measures or a combined score. The 2023 series is pre-BNS; post-July-2024 data needs a reviewed IPC/BNS mapping.

Validation and corrections

Inputs require registered sources and reviewed controls. Invalid schemas, personal-data headers, negative counts, duplicate identities and broken reconciliation are rejected. Changes pass pipeline checks before publication. No scraping, case-level data or personal records are used.
